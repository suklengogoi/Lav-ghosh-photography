The photographer

Lav Ghosh

Lav Ghosh Photography is a wedding photography and cinematography studio based in Barpeta Road, Assam. Since 2019, Lav and his team have photographed weddings across Assam and beyond.

The approach is cinematic and artistic, built on candid moments, elegant portraits and careful storytelling. From quiet, private emotions to grand celebrations, every detail is recorded so your memories feel real and look refined.

Natural emotion, thoughtful composition. Each photograph and film is made to reflect what your celebration was really like.
